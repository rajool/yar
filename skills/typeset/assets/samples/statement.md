## Basis

The statements are taken from the ledgers of the two entities of an illustrative group and shown at ledger line level: the entities as reported, their sum, the consolidation eliminations, and the consolidated result. Amounts are rounded to the unit; negatives in parentheses; nil as a dash. Every figure here is invented for the sample.
